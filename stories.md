# Work Item 5: Significant Movements and Explanations

For each stock we found the biggest single-day move (July 2024 - June 2025) and researched
a possible business reason. Sources are cited for each event. Note: news dates and numbers
match our data, but price data alone cannot prove causation.

## 1. SBUX +24.50% on 2024-08-13

Starbucks announced Brian Niccol (former Chipotle CEO) as its new CEO. SBUX jumped 24.50%;
CMG fell about 7.5% the same day on losing its CEO.
Company-specific event, fits the weak SBUX-CMG correlation (0.22).
Source: Investopedia, Markets News, Aug 13 2024 - https://www.investopedia.com/dow-jones-today-08132024-8694394

## 2. CMG -7.87% on 2024-10-30

Chipotle Q3 2024: revenue $2.78B missed estimates ($2.82B); same-store sales +6% missed too.
CMG fell 7.87% the next day, its biggest down day in our data.
Company earnings event; CMG stayed weak in our monthly summary afterwards.
Source: Yahoo Finance, Oct 30 2024 - https://finance.yahoo.com/news/chipotle-stock-drops-after-q3-sales-miss-wall-street-estimates-215928280.html

## 3. AMZN -8.98% and SBUX -11.22% on 2025-04-03

Trump announced broad "reciprocal" tariffs on Apr 2 ("Liberation Day"); markets crashed Apr 3
(S&P 500 -4.8%, worst day since 2020). AMZN -8.98%, SBUX -11.22% in our data.
Macro event: two stocks fell together; AMZN's move is ~4x its daily std (2.18).
Source: Investopedia, Markets News, Apr 3 2025 - https://www.investopedia.com/dow-jones-today-04032025-11708250

## 4. AMZN +11.98% and CMG +10.77% on 2025-04-09

Trump announced a 90-day tariff pause on Apr 9; S&P 500 +9.5% (best day since 2008).
AMZN +11.98%, CMG +10.77% in our data, biggest up days for both.
Macro event, moves together; mirror image of the Apr 3 crash in our chart 2 zoom-in.
Source: Investopedia, Markets News, Apr 9 2025 - https://www.investopedia.com/dow-jones-today-04092025-11711763
